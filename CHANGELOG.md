# Changelog

Notable user-visible changes to Omawix are recorded here.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-09-16

Initial public release.

- Browse and filter the online ZIM catalog.
- Download, pause, resume, retry, and cancel archives with persisted aria2 state.
- Discover and manage local ZIM libraries, including split archives.
- Read archives through a localhost-only embedded Kiwix reader.
- Use the Omarchy bar panel for library status and quick access.
